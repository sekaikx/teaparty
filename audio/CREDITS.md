# Audio credits

Recorded sounds were cut and levelled by `tools/audio/import_recorded.py` (run it after
`synth_tea_audio.py`, which makes the rest). Everything else in `audio/` is synthesised by
`tools/audio/synth_tea_audio.py` or comes from the Woods project.
